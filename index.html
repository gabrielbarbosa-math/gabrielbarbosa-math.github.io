About

I work in algebraic geometry. More specifically, I study objects related to holomorphic foliations and their moduli spaces.

Currently, I am a postdoctoral researcher at UFMG, in Belo Horizonte, Brasil. From January to July 2026, I was at Università degli Studi di Bari Aldo Moro, in Bari, Italy, as a postdoc researcher.
